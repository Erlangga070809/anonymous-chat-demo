Privacy Policy

1. Account Data

We collect your email address and password hash for account authentication. Your password is encrypted using bcrypt and is never stored in plaintext.

2. Anonymous ID

You are assigned a random anonymous ID. This ID is used to identify you to other users without revealing your real identity.

3. Messages

Messages exchanged during chats are stored in our database to enable real-time communication. You can delete your own messages, which marks them as deleted.

4. Media

Images and voice messages you send are stored on our servers. These files are accessible only to participants in the chat.

5. Technical Data

We may collect technical information such as IP address for security purposes, including preventing abuse and maintaining service integrity.

6. Cookies and Sessions

We use HTTP-only cookies to maintain your session. These cookies are essential for authentication and cannot be accessed by client-side scripts.

7. Usage Data

We collect basic usage statistics to improve our service. This includes matchmaking attempts, chat durations, and feature usage.

8. Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. Deleted accounts have their data permanently removed from our systems.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from settings. This permanently removes your account data, messages, and settings.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure cookies, and rate limiting to protect your data.

11. Third-Party Services

We use Neon PostgreSQL for database storage. Your data is stored on their secure infrastructure.

12. User Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at privacy@anonymous-chat.com.
